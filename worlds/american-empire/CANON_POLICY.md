# American Empire — Canon Authority and Additive Change Policy (P1)

## Source of truth

The canonical literary foundation is **The Quiet Accession** written archive. Its Universe Bible and Reality Matrix define history, major factions, imperial Stack, and constraints. Recovered Chronicles 02–04 tell narratives inside that foundation. Read all files in the pinned order. Never rewrite original bytes.

The timeline is a *fictional* branch after the August 31, 2026 reality cutoff: the late-2026 armistice and escalation to Dollar Night (2027), then world accession in 2037. Treat the hidden Continuity Directorate and its actions as fiction, **not** claims about actual institutions.

## Authority layers

1. **P0 original recovered canon** — pinned Universe Bible and Reality Matrix; source chronology and established facts in archived chronicles. Immutable source snapshot.
2. **Recovered proposal text** — chronicle sections explicitly headed "Additive canon proposed" / "Proposed additive canon" remain historical proposal text. Not automatically accepted, even though their manuscript bytes are preserved.
3. **Approved addenda** — future separately versioned canon entries, requiring explicit user approval, provenance, causal/temporal checks and an exact parent canon reference. May add detail; may not covertly replace P0.
4. **Adaptations** — the Paris Act I film scripts, images and media, and other visual storytelling. These can live *inside* the universe but have no authority to revise it. Compatibility is not promotion.
5. **Scenarios/simulation states** — counterfactual branches and computed outcomes. Never back-write their facts into canonical historical state.

## Canon invariants

- Years **2026–2037** and their established major event ordering remain locked without a specific user-directed revision.
- Continuity Directorate: Surveyors, Factors, Wardens, Keepers, Ascendants. Rival actors and independence movements retain their divergent incentives.
- Imperial Stack: **Atlas, Mesh, Grid, Forge, Sentinel, Covenant**.
- Main written viewpoints: **Mara Venn, Caleb Reed, Dr. Neda Farzan, Lin Meiyu, Jonah Vale**. They hold different incomplete truths.
- Central ambiguity: preservation of material life through an engineered one-way dependency; rescue does not equal free consent.
- No instant magical superintelligence, simple universal villain, cultural homogenization or unfounded omniscient viewpoint.
- Missing full standalone chronicles for later years must be marked **unwritten**, never generated and labeled "recovered".

## Review/promotion contract

A proposed change must declare: \`id\`, provenance locator and pinned parent, date/geography/entities impacted, factual claims, affected relationships, contradiction check, alternate scenario or adaptation linkage, and \`user_approval\` including the specific accepted scope. Default state: **candidate**. Until authorized, it does not change the authoritative Matrix or Bible.

Do not call a feature "canon" because it exists in a Drive video, GitHub song branch, image file, film storyboard, or an unreviewed continuation. Preserve records of disagreements and variants.

The pinned mirror is tamper-evident by Git object hashes and local verification, **not** server-side immutable storage. Changes to the lock or verifier themselves require review. Do not merge this development branch to GodRender \`main\` or render productions without separate review and GodRender gates.
