## Important distinction
The Quiet Accession is the larger written universe. The Paris `american-empire-act1` Scene 01–03 branches are a later cinematic production thread inside that universe; they do not replace the written bible or chronicles.

## Recovery rule
Preserve recovered source files verbatim. New scenes, treatments, or screenplay adaptations must be additive and must not silently rewrite recovered canon.
